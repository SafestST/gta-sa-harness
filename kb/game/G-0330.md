## Role
The **mission-script image archive** — 581,632 B. Companion to `main.scm`
(G-0329): holds additional scripted-asset payloads (scripted models/textures
that the SCM requests at runtime) which the engine streams on demand. Part of
B-G04 MISSION SCRIPT SYSTEM together with `data\main.sc` (G-0074).

## Structure
- Standard IMG dir2 archive (see G-0339): entry table (name/offset/size) ->
  RenderWare sections.
- Entry-list dump = the set of script-loaded assets.

## Relations
- main.scm (G-0329) drives which entries are requested; gta.dat (G-0070) lists
  `IMG DATA\SCRIPT\SCRIPT.IMG` as an auto-registered archive.
- SAMP does not extend this; it ships its own payload (samp.saa S-0013).

## Annotations
- P1 structural. Full per-entry asset decode follows the model pipeline rules
  in kb/game/B-MODELPIPE.md.
