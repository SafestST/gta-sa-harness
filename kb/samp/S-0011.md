## Role
The **SAMP mod core** — the native DLL that carries the multiplayer client.
Hooked/loaded into `gta_sa.exe` when launched via `samp.exe`. Implements the
network client (UDP to SA-MP servers), the in-game chat/UI (sampgui.png),
world-object overlay (SAMP\*.ide/ipl/img), and server-command surface (RCON).
